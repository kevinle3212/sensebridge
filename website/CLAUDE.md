# CLAUDE.md — website/

`website/` is a separate design surface from the app: brand register, web
platform, pre-launch. Four principles govern it — honesty over hype (never
imply the app is available, never break safety-framing), screen-reader-first as
a first-class requirement, restraint over conversion pressure (nothing is being
sold), and transparency about pre-launch status.

Its context is [`.agents/context/PRODUCT.md`](../.agents/context/PRODUCT.md) and
[`.agents/context/DESIGN.md`](../.agents/context/DESIGN.md) — not
[`docs/PRODUCT.md`](../docs/PRODUCT.md), which is the **app's** product doc. They
live under `.agents/` because that is where the `impeccable` skill reads them
from (see [`docs/TOOLING.md`](../docs/TOOLING.md) → "Impeccable project root").
